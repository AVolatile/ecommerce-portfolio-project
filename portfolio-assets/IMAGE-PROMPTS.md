# Original image generation / VELT

All three source assets were created with the built-in image generation tool, then copied into this project. Image preparation slices the regular catalog contact sheet and optimizes WebP assets; it does not introduce new product claims.

## Hero / product-mockup

Premium consumer brand VELT homepage hero, wide landscape photograph. A photorealistic editorial studio photograph of a charcoal graphite technical 24L backpack leaning on a raw concrete block on the right two-thirds. Squared architectural silhouette, textured ballistic nylon, small orange zip pull, tiny woven VELT label, two zipper compartments, folded front pocket, realistic straps. Pale concrete wall, hard afternoon sun, angled shadows. Product fully visible; left third empty for page typography. Tactile manufacturing detail. No mountains, camping, advertising text, or website mockup.

Source: `image-source/hero.png`. Derivatives: `public/images/hero.webp`, `p02-transit-pack.webp`, `og.jpg`.

## Catalog / product-mockup

An exact regular 3-column × 4-row contact sheet of twelve separate premium VELT utility products, each centered in an equal square with pale mineral-gray background and consistent studio side lighting. Row 1: 18L day pack, 24L commuter pack, 35L clamshell travel pack. Row 2: graphite weekender, petrol gym duffel, two packing cubes. Row 3: opened tech organizer, clay zip pouch, graphite steel bottle. Row 4: mineral overshirt, petrol lightweight shell, clay five-panel cap. Whole products with generous margins, tactile nylon, subtle orange pulls, no headings or ad copy.

Source: `image-source/catalog.png`. Derivatives: twelve catalog images; P—02 is overridden with a matching crop of the hero asset for identity consistency.

## Transit / photorealistic-natural

Candid rear three-quarter view of an adult professional wearing dark trousers, mineral overshirt, and charcoal squared technical backpack with small orange pulls, walking beside an architectural concrete train station. Afternoon light, real movement, bag sharp and tactile, face turned away. Muted analog photographic direction, architectural shadows, subject right of center. No mountains, staged influencers, or advertising overlays.

Source: `image-source/lifestyle.png`. Derivative: `public/images/lifestyle.webp`.
